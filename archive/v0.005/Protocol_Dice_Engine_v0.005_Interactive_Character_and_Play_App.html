Skills

Talents

Choose three starting Talents. Talents are standalone; they are not a class tree.

Declare a Talent at its listed Trigger, pay its Cost immediately, and apply only its stated Effect. Ordinary Edge Dice do not stack. Talents cannot retroactively create access, targets, knowledge, perception, consent, or physical possibility.

Magic

Magic uses Practice Skills and the normal core roll. Immediate magic acts within a scene; Ritual magic extends scale, duration, reach, or precision; Downtime magic creates durable preparations.

Gear & Edges

The single strongest relevant source normally provides the fifth Edge Die. Ordinary Edge Dice do not stack.

Advancement

Each Advancement Award grants 2 Skill Ranks. Every third Award also grants 1 Talent.
