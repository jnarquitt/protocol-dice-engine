# Dice Goblin — Future Physical Accessory Brand Note r001

**Status:** PARKED FOR LATER DEVELOPMENT

## Brand separation

- **Protocol Dice** is the tabletop RPG dice engine / rules brand.
- **Dice Goblin** is reserved as a physical tabletop accessory brand.

## Product direction

Dice Goblin products should focus on 3D-printed play aids for games that use large dice pools, especially mixed polyhedral pools.

Early concepts include:

- dice-pool trays with individual slots or channels;
- tubes, rails, or racks that let players lay out a full dice pool clearly before rolling;
- organizers that keep d4s easy to access and pick up, addressing the awkward shape and low profile of traditional tetrahedral d4s;
- holders that separate Ability, Skill, gear, feature, Mastery, or other dice by role;
- compact tabletop containers for storing and staging the dice needed for a character's current pool;
- modular pieces that can expand as a player's pool grows.

## Design intent

The purpose is practical accessibility and faster play, not decorative dice storage alone. Products should make large dice pools easier to assemble, read, pick up, organize, and reset between rolls.

This note is intentionally parked and should not interrupt current Protocol Dice / Protocol 7 rules development.
