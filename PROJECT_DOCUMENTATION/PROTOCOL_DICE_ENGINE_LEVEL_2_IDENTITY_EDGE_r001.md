# Protocol Dice Engine — Level 2 Identity Edge r001

**Status:** DESIGN CANDIDATE — simulation required

## Purpose

Early advancement needs a small universal moment of character identity that is mechanically useful without becoming a class, archetype, path, or prerequisite tree.

Working term: **Edge**. Final terminology is not locked.

An Edge says something distinctive about how a character operates. It never restricts later Skills, Features, Mastery, Capacity choices, or narrative identity. Implementations may rename, reskin, or expand the list.

## Acquisition

In a six-Level implementation using the current Protocol 7 advancement cadence, the first Edge is gained at **Level 2**.

The generic Engine should ultimately describe acquisition in terms of advancement milestones rather than require numbered Levels.

## Candidate Edge chassis

Each Edge provides one small persistent identity benefit or one **once-per-session** resource. The benefit should be noticeable, simple, and substantially weaker than Skill Mastery.

Candidate mechanical forms for testing:

- **Reroll:** once per session, reroll one die you rolled; keep the new result.
- **Push:** once per session, add d4 to one eligible roll after seeing the initial dice, subject to normal dice-source/ceiling rules.
- **Preparation:** once per session, establish one small plausible preparation, resource, or readiness benefit bounded by prior access and the fiction.
- **Connection:** once per session, invoke a plausible useful relationship/contact appropriate to the implementation and current situation.
- **Durability:** forgo the session resource and permanently increase maximum HP by a small fixed amount; **+5 HP is the Protocol 7 simulation candidate**, not yet a generic Engine constant.

## Identity principle

An Edge is not a class. It should be legal to rename/reskin an Edge to fit the character. Two characters may use the same mechanical Edge while describing it completely differently.

## Guardrails

- An Edge may not grant routine Skill Mastery.
- An Edge may not increase baseline AP or Reaction count.
- An added die follows the Engine's dice-source and practical-ceiling rules.
- Once-per-session resources should require almost no bookkeeping beyond whether they have been used.
- No Edge should become a prerequisite for later advancement.
- Edge selection should not determine or constrain a character's future role.

## Simulation questions

- Is once per session frequent enough to feel relevant?
- Does rerolling one die outperform adding d4 too consistently?
- Is +5 maximum HP competitive with session abilities without becoming mandatory for low-STR characters?
- Does the identity choice make an otherwise quiet early advancement feel meaningful?
- Should the resource refresh per session, mission, or operation?
- Does the added d4 create undesirable seven-die pools at veteran Levels, and if so should it replace another bonus die rather than stack?
