Open Frame

The Engine supports modern occult and archaic fantasy at the same table language. Skills include blades and firearms. Your world decides what is common. Characters are not class-locked. Archetypes are rapid Level 1 presets only — leveling opens the whole catalog.

Protocol Dice

Faces 1–3 are blank. Faces 4+ score their value. Roll the full pool together.

Normal pool: Skill die + the three Ability dice listed for that Skill.

Advantage: add d6. Disadvantage: remove the smallest Ability die. They cancel one-for-one; each side caps at one effective instance.

Ties reroll (exact match to target or opposed total).

No routine flat numeric modifiers. Named dice sources only.

Spells are skills

Spellcasting is a Skill (INT · WIS · CHA). Ritual covers slow workings. Spells are effects you cast by rolling that skill — same Protocol Dice math.

Prepare spells into Capacity (shared with Features). Casting costs AP and a roll vs Difficulty or an opposed defense. There are no Vancian slots and no detached mana track.

Features & Capacity

Features are the special-resource layer (Protocol 7 called these VAMs). Capacity at Level 1 is 16. Loaded Features and prepared spells both spend Capacity.

Vitality

Roll each Ability die once. Starting HP = 18 + highest face of 4+. If all six faces are blank, starting HP = 28. Each level after 1: +4 max HP and +4 Skill Ranks.

Play & combat

When you do not know what to do, go to Play. Pools are assembled and rolled only there.

3 AP per turn. Normal attack 1 AP. Move 30 feet per AP. Active defense: both sides roll; damage equals winning margin if the attacker wins. Armor adds a gear die to defense rather than subtracting damage afterward (when gear dice are in play).

One Reaction between turns, spending banked AP first.

Archetypes

Street Adept, Occult Scholar, Oathbound Blade, Fixer, Hunter, Tinker — start packages only. Opt out and build custom at any time.
